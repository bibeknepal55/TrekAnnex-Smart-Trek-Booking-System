Our Story

Founded in 2020, Trek Annex was born from a passion for the mountains and a desire to share the incredible beauty of Nepal with the world. Our team of experienced mountaineers and local guides brings decades of combined expertise to ensure every trek is safe, memorable, and transformative.

We've grown from a small team of 5 to a family of 30+ dedicated professionals, but our core values remain unchanged: safety, sustainability, and authentic experiences.
